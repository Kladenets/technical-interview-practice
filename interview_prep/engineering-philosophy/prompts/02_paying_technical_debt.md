# Deciding when to pay debt

**Level:** senior
**Time:** 20m

How do you decide when technical debt gets paid rather than worked around? Give an example where you made that call and explain what you chose not to do.

## Cover
- Your decision criteria and claim about debt
- Evidence from a specific project
- Costs, opportunity cost, and affected people
- How the decision showed up in planning

## Stretch
- Describe when visible debt should still wait.
