# Changing a technical opinion

**Level:** staff
**Time:** 25m

Tell me about a technical opinion you changed your mind about. What evidence changed it, what did the old view cost, and how did you help others adjust?

## Cover
- The former and current positions
- Concrete evidence and lived consequences
- Conditions and organisational context
- A changed practice or team mechanism

## Stretch
- Describe how you would detect that your new view has become stale.
