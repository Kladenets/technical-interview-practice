# How You Build Software

**Level:** senior
**Time:** 25m
**Context:** mongoose

Walk me through a recent feature from brief to production as you actually built it. Where did you begin, how did you break the work down, test and review it, ship it, and determine whether it worked?

## Cover
- A specific recent feature and the position or decision principle that guided your first step.
- The real tradeoff you made while decomposing, implementing, testing, or reviewing it.
- The conditions that would have made you choose a different process or level of rigor.
- The concrete delivery and validation behaviours you used, including the observed outcome.

## Stretch
- Explain what you would change on a nine-person startup team versus a large organisation, and the cost of that change.
