# Abstraction or duplication

**Level:** senior
**Time:** 20m

When do you abstract, and when do you deliberately duplicate? Use a real design choice to explain the signals you use and the failure you are trying to avoid.

## Cover
- Your falsifiable rule of thumb
- Specific context and consequences
- The cost of your choice
- Conditions that reverse the choice

## Stretch
- Explain how you would help a team apply the rule consistently.
