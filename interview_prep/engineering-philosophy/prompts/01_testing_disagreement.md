# Testing belief your team disputed

**Level:** senior
**Time:** 20m

What do you believe about testing that your last team disagreed with? Take a position, then tell me what experience formed it and how you applied it when others preferred a different approach.

## Cover
- Your specific claim and the decision it changes
- A concrete incident that formed or tested it
- The cost and who bore it
- Conditions that would change your view

## Stretch
- Explain how you would turn the position into a team norm without making it dogma.
