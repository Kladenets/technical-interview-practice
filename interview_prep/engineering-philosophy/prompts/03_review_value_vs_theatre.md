# Valuable review versus theatre

**Level:** senior
**Time:** 20m

What makes a code review valuable rather than theatre? Describe a review practice you would defend, the cost it creates, and a time you changed it.

## Cover
- A clear claim about review purpose
- A lived example and outcome
- Review friction or delay you accept
- Practical behaviours in PRs and planning

## Stretch
- Say how your approach changes for a new or struggling team.
