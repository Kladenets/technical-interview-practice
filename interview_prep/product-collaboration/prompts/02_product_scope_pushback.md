# Pushing Back on Product Scope

**Level:** senior
**Time:** 20m
**Context:** mongoose

Tell me about a time you pushed back on product scope. What outcome did the product manager want, what evidence changed your view of the proposed solution, and what did you offer instead?

## Cover
- The named user need or business outcome behind the request.
- The cost, risk, or constraint that made the original scope a poor choice.
- A concrete alternative, what it intentionally excluded, and how you negotiated the decision.
- What customer evidence or result showed whether the compromise was right.

## Stretch
- Explain how the decision changed a later roadmap or planning conversation.
