# Conflicting Needs Across User Classes

**Level:** senior
**Time:** 20m
**Context:** onsemble

Tell me about a time two distinct user groups wanted the product to go in different directions — for example an independent operator versus an internal operations team. How did you decide whether a request was a local one-off or a reusable product capability, and what did you ship?

## Cover
- The two user groups, the conflicting requests, and the underlying problem each was really trying to solve
- The customer feedback, support evidence, or metric you used to weigh the requests rather than deciding on volume alone
- How you decided one-off versus reusable capability, and the bounded slice you proposed to reach the outcome
- How you sequenced delivery and confirmed the shipped result actually solved the customer problem for both groups or explicitly for one

## Stretch
- Explain how you turned a single customer's request into a capability others adopted without forking the product for each
