# Driving Work After Handoff

**Level:** senior
**Time:** 20m
**Context:** mongoose

Tell me about an initiative handed to you with no one directing what happened next. How did you decide what to investigate, decide alone, and bring back to product before you shipped?

## Cover
- The incomplete intent, named user, and assumptions you needed to test.
- The actions you took to unblock yourself and the decisions you made without waiting.
- The uncertainty you escalated, why it mattered, and how you framed the product choice.
- How you sequenced delivery and checked that the result solved the customer problem.

## Stretch
- Explain how your approach created clarity or momentum for others, not just your own task.
