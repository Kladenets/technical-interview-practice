# Being Wrong About a User Need

**Level:** senior
**Time:** 15m
**Context:** mongoose

Tell me about a time you were wrong about what a user needed. How did you discover the mistake, change the product or implementation, and make the next decision less assumption-driven?

## Cover
- The user, original assumption, and product decision it influenced.
- The customer feedback, support evidence, or metric that proved the assumption wrong.
- How you translated the learning into a revised scope or technical approach.
- The release decision you made and how you verified the correction.

## Stretch
- Explain how you built a feedback loop that helped product and engineering catch comparable assumptions earlier.
