# From Vague Brief to Shipped Feature

**Level:** senior
**Time:** 25m
**Context:** mongoose

Tell me about a vague product brief you took to a shipped feature. Walk me through the actual path from handoff to production, including the questions you asked before writing code and the evidence that told you it worked.

## Cover
- The user problem, unknowns, and real constraint you excavated from the brief.
- How you independently turned those answers into a technical and delivery plan.
- What you sliced for the first release and the user or business rationale for deferring work.
- The customer feedback or metric used to validate the shipped result.

## Stretch
- Explain a discovery or delivery mechanism you left behind that made the next product handoff better.
