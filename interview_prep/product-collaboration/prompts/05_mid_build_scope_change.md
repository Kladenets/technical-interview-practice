# Handling a Mid-Build Scope Change

**Level:** senior
**Time:** 20m
**Context:** mongoose

Describe a feature whose scope changed while you were building it. How did you determine whether the new request was essential, renegotiate the plan, and protect a useful release?

## Cover
- The user signal or business change behind the new request.
- The delivery, technical, and customer consequences you made visible to product.
- The scope or sequencing alternatives you offered and the decision reached.
- What shipped when, what was deferred, and the evidence used to judge the outcome.

## Stretch
- Describe how you changed discovery or change-control practices to reduce this kind of surprise.
