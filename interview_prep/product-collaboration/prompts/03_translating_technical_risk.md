# Translating Technical Risk

**Level:** senior
**Time:** 20m
**Context:** mongoose

Describe a time a non-technical stakeholder wanted an approach you believed carried a meaningful technical cost or risk. How did you explain the choice so they could decide, and what happened?

## Cover
- The stakeholder's desired outcome and the user or customer affected.
- The technical constraint translated into delivery, reliability, compliance, or business consequences.
- The options you presented, including their costs and what you recommended.
- How you used evidence to validate the eventual decision.

## Stretch
- Describe how you made the tradeoff visible for similar decisions after this incident.
