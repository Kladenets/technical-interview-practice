# Data Trust With Stakeholders

**Level:** senior
**Time:** 20m
**Context:** link-logistics

Tell me about a time a business or analyst stakeholder stopped trusting a number your software produced — a stale value, a wrong metric, or an unexplained change. How did you find the cause, fix it, and rebuild their confidence in the data?

## Cover
- The stakeholder, the decision the number fed, and how the trust problem surfaced
- The evidence you used to trace the cause — lineage, freshness, a bad input, or a logic error — rather than guessing
- How you translated the technical cause and the fix into terms the stakeholder could act on
- What you changed so the same class of error would be caught earlier (provenance, alerting, an SLA, or a check)

## Stretch
- Explain how you made data trust a standing product requirement rather than a one-time fix
