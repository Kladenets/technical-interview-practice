# Policy for AI-assisted contributions

**Level:** staff
**Time:** 25m

What would you put in a team policy for AI-assisted contributions? Focus on useful guardrails rather than a list of prohibitions.

## Cover
- Allowed use and boundaries
- Author ownership and verification expectations
- Sensitive code, licensing, and data concerns
- Review, onboarding, and enforcement effects

## Stretch
- Explain how you would evaluate whether the policy improves outcomes.
