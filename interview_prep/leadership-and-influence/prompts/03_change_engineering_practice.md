# Changing an Engineering Practice

**Level:** senior
**Time:** 30m

Tell me about changing an engineering practice or process that was not working. What made people adopt the change rather than merely comply?

## Cover
- Set the failure mode, affected scope, and cost of retaining the old practice
- Describe the technical direction and alternatives considered
- Explain the influence mechanism, rollout, objections, and adoption cost
- Show measurable effects and the mechanism that made the change durable

## Stretch
- Explain how you avoided becoming the owner or bottleneck for the practice
