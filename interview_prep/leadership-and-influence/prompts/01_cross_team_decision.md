# A Cross-team Decision Without Authority

**Level:** senior
**Time:** 30m

Tell me about driving a technical decision across teams when you did not manage the people involved. How did you get from disagreement to action?

## Cover
- Define the teams, conflicting incentives, stakes, and scope of influence
- State the direction, alternatives, and migration or business tradeoffs
- Explain the actual alignment mechanism: RFC, data, prototype, sequencing, or negotiation
- Show outcomes, adoption, and what outlasted your involvement

## Stretch
- Explain how you reduced each team's migration cost rather than just winning the argument
