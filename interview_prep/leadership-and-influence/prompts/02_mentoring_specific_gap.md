# Mentoring Through a Specific Gap

**Level:** senior
**Time:** 25m

Tell me about mentoring someone through a specific technical or leadership gap. How did you diagnose the need and know the support worked?

## Cover
- Describe the person's context, the observed gap, and the stakes
- Explain your diagnosis and why you chose this intervention over alternatives
- Detail the progression, feedback, risk management, and transfer of ownership
- Give evidence of their outcome and the longer-term effect on the team

## Stretch
- Show how you turned the approach into a repeatable mentoring practice
