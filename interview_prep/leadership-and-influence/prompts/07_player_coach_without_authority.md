# Player-Coach Without Authority

**Level:** senior
**Time:** 20m
**Context:** link-logistics

Tell me about a time you were responsible for the quality of work delivered by people you did not manage — contractors, interns, or less-experienced engineers — while still writing significant code yourself. How did you raise their output without formal authority?

## Cover
- The situation, who you were responsible for, and the quality or delivery risk you were accountable for
- The mechanism you used to raise the bar — code review standards, examples, pairing, or a written norm — rather than just doing the work yourself
- How you balanced your own delivery against coaching, and what you chose to keep versus delegate
- A concrete outcome: how the contributors' work or the team's standard measurably improved
- How you handled a case where the quality bar was not being met

## Stretch
- Explain how you left behind a standard or practice that outlasted your direct involvement
