# Setting Direction for an Area

**Level:** staff
**Time:** 40m

Imagine you are asked to set technical direction for an area over the next 12–18 months, spanning several teams. How would you form the strategy and turn it into progress without centralising every decision?

## Cover
- Define the area boundary, stakeholders, current constraints, and desired outcomes
- Present principles, roadmap choices, tradeoffs, and migration strategy
- Explain mechanisms for alignment, local autonomy, investment decisions, and conflict resolution
- Describe talent development, success measures, stakeholder communication, and durable ownership

## Stretch
- Explain how the strategy changes when business priorities or staffing assumptions move
