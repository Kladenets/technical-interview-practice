# Inheriting a System in Bad Shape

**Level:** staff
**Time:** 35m

Tell me about inheriting a system or team in bad shape. How did you stabilise it while creating a direction that others could sustain?

## Cover
- Set the operational, people, and customer stakes and the initial scope
- Describe your prioritisation principles and rejected approaches
- Explain alignment, ownership changes, staffing, and communication mechanisms
- Show improvements in reliability, capability, and durable organisational leverage

## Stretch
- Explain how multiple teams or leaders contributed to and owned the new direction
