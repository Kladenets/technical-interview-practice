# Communicating Technical Risk

**Level:** senior
**Time:** 25m

Tell me about communicating a technical risk to non-technical leadership when the right response required a difficult investment or tradeoff.

## Cover
- Define the risk, uncertainty, affected customers or business outcome, and decision needed
- Explain the options, technical direction, and what you recommended
- Show how you translated evidence for the audience and handled objections
- Give the outcome, follow-through mechanism, and what you would improve

## Stretch
- Include how you made the risk visible over time rather than relying on one presentation
