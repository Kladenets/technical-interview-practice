# Breadth Under Ownership

**Level:** senior
**Time:** 20m

Tell me about owning a feature end to end across parts of the stack outside your strongest area. How did you become competent quickly, decide when your work was good enough to ship, and recognise when to bring in help?

## Cover
- A bounded, specific feature and the unfamiliar area where you had a real gap.
- Evidence for the actions you took to learn, validate your work, and the resulting outcome.
- The decision criteria and tradeoff behind calling the work good enough versus seeking help.
- A genuine limitation or mistake, its consequence, and the feedback practice you used to improve.

## Stretch
- Connect this incident to a deliberate career direction and the breadth you want to develop next.
