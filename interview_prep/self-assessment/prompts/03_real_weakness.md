# A real weakness and its cost

**Level:** senior
**Time:** 20m

What is a real weakness in how you work, and what has it cost? Tell me what you are doing about it and how you know whether that is working.

## Cover
- An honest, non-humblebrag limitation
- A named consequence and situation
- Current remediation practice
- A feedback signal or measure

## Stretch
- Explain how you prevent the weakness harming collaborators while improving.
