# Why This Environment

**Level:** senior
**Time:** 15m
**Context:** link-logistics

Why is a senior individual-contributor role at a large, digitally-native company — one where technology is treated as a strategic advantage rather than a cost centre — the right next step for you, versus a startup or a pure platform role? Ground the answer in a real tradeoff.

## Cover
- Specific career direction and the role needs that make this environment the credible next step
- Situational evidence that you deliver in ambiguity and across a broad stack, with real stakeholder contact
- A genuine difficulty you expect — scale, in-office expectations, or coordinating across business and data teams — and its likely consequence
- A concrete way you would get feedback and adapt if that difficulty appeared

## Stretch
- Explain what you are deliberately choosing not to optimise for by taking this kind of role now
