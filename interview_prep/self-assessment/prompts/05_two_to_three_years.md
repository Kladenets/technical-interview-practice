# Where you want to be

**Level:** senior
**Time:** 20m

Where do you want to be in two to three years, and what is missing today? Connect the answer to choices you are making now rather than a title alone.

## Cover
- A specific direction and rationale
- Current evidence and gaps
- Concrete development actions
- Why this role fits next

## Stretch
- Name a capability you will stop prioritising to make room.
