# Why This Stage

**Level:** senior
**Time:** 15m
**Context:** mongoose

Why is an early-stage startup the right next step for you now? Ground your answer in a real tradeoff: what you want that a larger company cannot give you, what you are knowingly giving up, and what might be hard about joining as engineer number eight.

## Cover
- Specific career direction and the role needs that make this the credible next step.
- Situational evidence that you have worked effectively with ambiguity and broad ownership.
- A genuine limitation or difficulty you expect in this environment and its likely consequence.
- A concrete way you would get feedback and adapt if that difficulty appeared.

## Stretch
- Explain what you are deliberately choosing not to optimise for at this stage of your career.
