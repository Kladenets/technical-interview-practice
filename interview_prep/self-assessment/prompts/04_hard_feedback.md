# Feedback that was hard to hear

**Level:** senior
**Time:** 20m

Tell me about feedback that was difficult to hear. Why was it accurate or inaccurate, what did you change, and what evidence tells you the change lasted?

## Cover
- Specific feedback and context
- Honest initial reaction and evaluation
- Changed behaviour
- Evidence of impact over time

## Stretch
- Describe what you would do if the feedback conflicted with your self-image again.
