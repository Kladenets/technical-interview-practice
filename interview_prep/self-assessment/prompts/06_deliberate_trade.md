# What you choose not to be good at

**Level:** staff
**Time:** 25m

What are you deliberately choosing not to be good at, and why is that the right trade? Describe how you compensate so the choice does not become a blind spot.

## Cover
- A real, bounded trade-off
- Effects on people and systems
- Evidence from a career situation
- Structural compensation and feedback

## Stretch
- Explain how this choice supports the scope you want next.
