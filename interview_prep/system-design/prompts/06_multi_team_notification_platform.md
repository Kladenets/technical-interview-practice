# Multi-team Notification Platform

**Level:** staff
**Time:** 55m

Several product teams send email, push, and in-app notifications through separate services with inconsistent preferences and delivery tracking. Design a shared platform without forcing every team into the same release schedule.

## Cover
- Define product boundaries, tenant needs, delivery guarantees, and excluded capabilities
- Explain send, preference-check, provider delivery, and status-query flows
- Deep-dive idempotency, provider failure handling, and preference consistency
- Address scale, cost, observability, ownership boundaries, and staged adoption

## Stretch
- Propose an API and migration strategy that preserves team autonomy
- State reusable principles for future delivery channels
