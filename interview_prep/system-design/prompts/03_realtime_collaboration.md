# Realtime Collaborative Editing

**Level:** senior
**Time:** 50m

Design collaborative editing for shared documents with cursors and near-realtime updates. Users may edit offline briefly and reconnect to a document with active collaborators.

## Cover
- Clarify concurrency, conflict, durability, latency, and document-size assumptions
- Describe connection, edit, persistence, and replay flows
- Deep-dive the concurrency or conflict-resolution model
- Explain reconnect behaviour, hotspots, and operational signals

## Stretch
- State what correctness guarantees are deliberately out of scope
- Describe how you would evolve the edit protocol without disconnecting clients
