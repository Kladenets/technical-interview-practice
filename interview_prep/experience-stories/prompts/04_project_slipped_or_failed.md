# A Project That Slipped

**Level:** senior
**Time:** 25m

Tell me about a project that slipped or failed. What did you own, what signals did you miss, and how did you handle the consequences?

## Cover
- Describe the original commitment, dependencies, and cost of failure
- Explain your actions, tradeoffs, and alternatives at the decision points
- Show how you communicated risk and changed execution as facts emerged
- Quantify the impact and give a specific counterfactual

## Stretch
- Describe a lasting planning or delivery mechanism created from the failure
