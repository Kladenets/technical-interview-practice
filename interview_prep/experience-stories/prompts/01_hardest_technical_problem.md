# Hardest Technical Problem

**Level:** senior
**Time:** 25m

Tell me about the hardest technical problem you have solved. What made it difficult, and what did you personally do?

## Cover
- Set the situation, stakes, constraints, and your specific ownership
- Describe the options you considered and why you rejected alternatives
- Explain execution, risks, and how you influenced others
- Quantify the result and give a genuine counterfactual

## Stretch
- Show how the solution or lesson became reusable beyond your immediate team
