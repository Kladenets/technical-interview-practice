# A Decision You Got Wrong

**Level:** senior
**Time:** 25m

Tell me about a technical decision you made that turned out to be wrong. How did you recognise it and what did you do next?

## Cover
- State the original context, stakes, decision, and your role
- Explain viable alternatives and the criteria that led you to the wrong choice
- Describe detection, mitigation, communication, and risk management
- Give measurable consequences and what you would change next time

## Stretch
- Explain the mechanism you introduced so others can detect or avoid the same error
