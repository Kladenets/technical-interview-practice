# Work Nobody Asked For

**Level:** staff
**Time:** 30m

Tell me about useful work you did that nobody explicitly asked you to do. Why was it worth prioritising, and how did you bring others with you?

## Cover
- Frame the neglected problem, stakeholders, and consequences of inaction
- Describe your personal initiative and alternatives you declined
- Explain sequencing, resistance, risk, and influence without formal authority
- Quantify outcomes and what you would change in hindsight

## Stretch
- Show how the work became a durable mechanism or changed multiple teams' behaviour
