# Technical Disagreement

**Level:** senior
**Time:** 25m

Tell me about a time you disagreed with a peer or manager on a technical call. How did you reach a decision when the stakes were real?

## Cover
- Establish the disagreement, constraints, decision owner, and impact
- Separate your personal position and actions from the team's outcome
- Compare alternatives and evidence used to resolve the disagreement
- Quantify the result and name what you would do differently

## Stretch
- Show how you preserved trust and made later disagreements easier to handle
