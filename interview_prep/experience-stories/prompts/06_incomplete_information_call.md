# A Call Under Incomplete Information

**Level:** staff
**Time:** 30m

Tell me about making a consequential call before you had all the information you wanted. How did you bound the risk and decide when to revisit it?

## Cover
- State the uncertainty, stakes, time pressure, and decision authority
- Explain your personal reasoning, alternatives, and decision criteria
- Describe safeguards, communication, and how you adapted to new evidence
- Quantify the outcome and give the counterfactual you now use

## Stretch
- Connect the decision to customer, business, cost, or risk outcomes across teams
