# Strategy — Link Logistics

## Source of truth

- Role-specific context: `interview-brief.md`, `job-description.md` in this
  directory.
- Current preparation priority: **prove full-stack analytics delivery and
  player-coach judgment.** The interview format is unconfirmed, so keep both a
  coding surface and a conversational surface warm until the loop is known.
- Referral first: use the internal contact to resolve the interview loop,
  in-office reality, and team shape before investing in the wrong stage.

## The through-line

Three claims, carried across every stage:

1. **I ship data-dense products end to end — React/TypeScript front end, Python/
   SQL back end — and I make the numbers trustworthy.** Their platform exists to
   give internal stakeholders insight they can act on; framing everything around
   analyst/business trust lands with an org that treats a stale signal as a
   production incident.
2. **I raise the bar for a team without needing the title.** The player-coach
   mandate is the differentiator in this posting. Founding the DevOps Guild,
   standardizing workflows, and reviewing code are the evidence — lead with them.
3. **I use AI-assisted tooling heavily and I am calibrated about it.** MCP servers
   and agentic workflows are ahead of the bar; the value is verification,
   ownership, and rollback, not velocity for its own sake.

If an answer does not reinforce one of these, it is filler.

## Planning rules

- Optimize for interview signal, not topic coverage.
- Replay weak areas before adding new scope.
- Prefer practical full-stack analytics slices over isolated algorithm volume.
- Every abstract claim needs a concrete incident: name the project, the
  constraint, and the outcome (52→1 deployments, 27-market regression, MTTR, 11%
  Geocoding cost, SSR state-leak fix).
- Quantify or cut.
- Do not overclaim Azure, Databricks, or the ML stack. Frame them as consume-and-
  integrate with an explicit ramp plan; let honesty plus the referral carry level.

## Known risk areas

- **Cloud/data mismatch (Azure/Databricks/Spark).** The biggest technical gap.
  Close the vocabulary gap and prepare a credible ramp story; do not fake depth.
- **ML-output integration and data provenance.** Learn to talk about freshness,
  confidence, lineage, and evals as first-class product requirements.
- **Player-coach without direct-report history.** Prepare specific mentoring and
  standards-setting stories; be honest about the management delta.
- **The 10+ year line.** Lead with scope and ownership; let the referral vouch.
- **Story recall.** Slowest to improve — start early, revisit often.
