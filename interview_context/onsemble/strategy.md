# Onsemble Strategy

## Source of truth

- Near-term priority: prepare for the Onsemble full-stack interview in the next 1–2 weeks
- Use `interview_context/onsemble/interview-brief.md` for company, role, and fit context
- Use `interview_context/onsemble/crash-course.md` for the current focused plan
- 10–11 hrs/week when the interview is still 1–2 weeks away; compress to 6–8 hrs if it is within 7 days
- Mon–Sat study, Sunday off
- Cover: role-shaped coding, practical system design, behavioral, AI-native engineering, and targeted outreach

## Planning rules

- Optimize for interview signal, not broad topic coverage.
- Weekly goals must be outcome-based and tied to the likely interview loop
- Replay weak areas before adding new scope
- Prefer practical full-stack slices over isolated algorithm volume
- Practice explaining tradeoffs, customer discovery, production ownership, and responsible AI use
- Keep outreach targeted: clarify the process and ask role-specific questions, not generic networking
- Return to the broader plan only after the Onsemble process is complete or paused

## Suggested cadence

- Mon–Thu: ~1.5h coding + ~15–30m secondary work
- Fri: ~1.5h system design + ~45m behavioral
- Sat: ~2h replay, review, outreach, mock
- Sun: off
