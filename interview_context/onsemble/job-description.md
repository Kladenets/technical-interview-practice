## Company and product context

Hajoca is a large, privately held wholesale distributor of plumbing, heating,
cooling, and industrial supplies. It operates in a decentralized model where
local Profit Centers run their businesses with substantial autonomy.

Onsemble is a digital innovation team inside Hajoca. The team operates with the
autonomy of a startup and the customer base and durability of a major
corporation. Its platform serves small and mid-sized contractors and Profit
Centers, with a focus on AI-enabled workflows that make real-world businesses
more productive, profitable, and future-ready.

## Role summary

The Full-Stack Engineer builds product across the contractor-facing platform,
partners closely with Product and Engineering leadership, translates real
customer needs into reliable and scalable software, and owns features
end-to-end across the stack.

The role emphasizes daily production delivery, architecture across frontend and
backend systems, and an AI-native development model in which agentic coding
tools are central to the development process.

## Responsibilities

### Product and execution

- Own product features from discovery through launch and iteration.
- Partner with Product to balance speed, quality, and durability.
- Ship production software that supports increasing scale, complexity, and adoption.
- Respond with urgency to important or time-sensitive matters and own their resolution.

### AI-native software development

- Use tools such as Claude Code and similar agentic coding systems to design, build, review, and ship production software.
- Use AI to increase leverage, velocity, and code quality while applying human judgment to architecture, correctness, and maintainability.
- Build AI-enabled features responsibly, pragmatically, and around real customer value.

### Technical contribution

- Contribute to frontend and backend architecture and technical decisions.
- Maintain a high bar for code quality, reliability, and maintainability.
- Improve development workflows, tooling, and processes to increase velocity.
- Identify risks early and surface them through planning and clear communication.
