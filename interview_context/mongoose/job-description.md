# Job Description — Mongoose

Captured from an initial recruiter/founder call plus the public product site.
Not an official posting; treat specifics as paraphrase.

## Role

Senior Software Engineer, full stack. One of two engineering hires taking the
team from ~7–8 to 9–10 by year end.

## Product

An AI experience layer for health insurance payers. Governed AI agents perform
the work behind each customer interaction rather than just answering questions —
e.g. a member reports a new child and the agent prepares the enrollment change,
ID cards, PCP selection, benefits update and follow-up scheduling.

Sold to payers; used by members, brokers, employers, providers, service-centre
representatives and operations teams. Also functions as a platform that the
payer's own engineering and business teams build on.

## Responsibilities

- Own features end to end — frontend, backend, release.
- Take initiatives from product handoff and drive them without close direction.
- Work directly with product managers, each owning a product area.
- Contribute across a frontend-heavy stack with some backend work.

## Stack

- Frontend-heavy web application.
- Some backend.
- Mobile via Flutter/Dart.
- Integrations across REST, GraphQL, FHIR and HL7.
- Claude and Cursor used daily.

## Ways of working

- Agile, multiple PMs by area.
- Long-term roadmap, reactive to customer needs.
- Remote, Eastern hours. Team concentrated in NJ/NY/New England.
- Quarterly in-person gatherings in New York or Connecticut.

## Compensation and benefits

- $180,000–$200,000.
- Health insurance — noted as more expensive given company size.
- Parental leave; founders carried the policy over from a previous startup.

## Interview process

No live coding interview and no take-home. Approximately two weeks.

1. Two live sessions with engineers on how you go about building software.
2. Conversation with the CTO — big picture.
3. Two final conversations with the founders (CEO and Chief Product Officer) on
   working with product and culture fit.

The CEO has previously used a short system design question to check hands-on
ability. How you use AI tools is an explicit topic.
