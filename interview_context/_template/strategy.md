## Planning rules

- Optimize for interview signal, not topic coverage.
- Replay weak areas before adding new scope. A `_v2` that moves a weak answer up
  is worth more than three fresh answers that stay mediocre.
- Prefer practical, role-shaped slices over isolated algorithm volume.
- Every abstract claim needs a concrete incident attached: name the project, the
  constraint, and the outcome.
- Quantify or cut. Unquantified results read below level.
